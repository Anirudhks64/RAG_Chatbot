Learn. Create. Solve with AI.

Building the next generation of AI creators

AI can code. AI can design. AI can build.
But it can’t decide what’s worth building.
It can’t tell when it’s wrong.
And it can’t care about the people it’s built for.

That’s the human part. That’s what we grow.


At AI Immersion Lab, young people from Grade 5 to Graduates turn their ideas into real projects through hands-on development, and grow the clear thinking and judgement to direct AI well. 


People who know how to work with AI will shape the future. Here, they start today.

Campus to Corporate

There’s a gap between what people study and what work actually demands. Employers look for proof that someone can take a real problem, work in a real role, and deliver. Campus to Corporate closes that gap with real projects, real roles and expert feedback, at whatever stage you’re at. 

 

How it works 

  1. Pick a project from a real industry.
  2. Take on a role, technical or non-technical, just as you would in a real team.
  3. Build and deliver, solo or as a team, on our platform.
  4. Prove it: present a live demo to industry experts for review and sign-off.

What you walk away with

  • A portfolio of real work you can show and share
  • Expert sign-off on what you delivered
  • A TransAccelors experience/internship letter for eligible participants
  • Real stories to tell in any interview or application
